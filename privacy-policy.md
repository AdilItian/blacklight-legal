# Blacklight Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

Blacklight ("the app") is a flashlight app for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **it collects nothing.**

## What we collect

Nothing. Blacklight does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it has no internet access, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

## What stays on your phone

The app remembers a few settings on your device only, such as whether "Shake to light" is switched on, which flashlight pattern (Beam) you last chose, the short Morse message you typed, and whether you have already seen its introductions and notices. This never leaves your phone, and uninstalling the app removes it.

## Permissions the app uses, and why

- **Camera flash (torch):** to turn your flashlight on and off. Blacklight does not use the camera to take photos or video and does not ask for the camera permission.
- **Motion sensor (accelerometer):** only while "Shake to light" is on, to notice when you shake the phone three times. The readings are used instantly to detect the shake and are never recorded or stored.
- **Notifications:** to show the small notification that Android requires while "Shake to light" or a blinking Beam is running. Each has a button to turn it off.
- **Foreground service, wake lock:** to keep listening for the shake, and to keep a blinking flashlight pattern (Hazard, SOS, Morse or Storm) running, while the screen is off.
- **Vibration:** a short buzz to confirm the flashlight turned on or off.
- **Run at start-up:** to start "Shake to light" again after you restart your phone, if you had it on.

You can turn "Shake to light" off, or stop a Beam, at any time, in the app or from its notification. The Quick Settings tile and the app-icon shortcut only switch the flashlight and need no extra permission.

## Children

Blacklight is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
